Olympic LOD — Tokyo 2020 Olympic Games as 5-star Linked Open Data

Daniel Samuel Edim · Ahmad Imran — Faculty of Engineering, University of Porto · release 1.0.0

The Kaggle Tokyo 2020 CSV dump turned into Linked Open Data: athletes, coaches, technical officials, teams, events and medals, with stable IRIs and links to Wikidata. The data is validated against its own application profile in SHACL.

Cite: doi:10.5281/zenodo.24543242

Files

  • tokyo2020.ttl — the dataset (Turtle, 109,679 triples, 7.8 MB)
  • oly.ttl — the ontology of the 9 minted terms, https://w3id.org/olympic-lod/ontology#
  • olympic-lod-shapes.ttl — the application profile as SHACL shapes
  • validation-report.csv — SHACL results (0 violations)
  • issues-log.csv — source problems kept and flagged for review
