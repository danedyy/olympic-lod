The **code** in this repository (`pipeline/`, `tests/`) is under the MIT licence ([`LICENSE`](LICENSE)).

The **data** is under **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International**
(CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/>). This covers:

* `data/source/`: the original CSV files, *Tokyo 2020 Olympic Summer Games* by Petro Ivaniuk,
  <https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/piterfm/tokyo-2020-olympics>, published under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0;
* `data/rdf/`, `ldap/`, `shacl/` and `ontology/`: the data and the application profile derived from it.
  ShareAlike requires the same licence.

The links to Wikidata come from Wikidata (CC0).

**Caveat.** The NonCommercial clause means the data does not meet the Open Definition, so it is
"open" only in a limited sense. The source sets this condition, and we cannot lift it.
